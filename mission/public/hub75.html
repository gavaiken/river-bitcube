An experiment, not the show. Only the moving water from the river footage, cut out, zoomed and drawn on four simulated HUB75 screens. Everything that doesn't move is sent as true black (LEDs off). Nothing here reaches bitcube.

The four screens

Each screen is 128 × 256 LEDs (four 128 × 64 panels, hung portrait). Drag to pan when zoomed.
