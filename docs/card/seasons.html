A River Becoming

Colin, Justine, Will, Dave and Gavin, with Andi and Lilian · 2026
Twenty ropes of light, LED panels, driftwood and river stones, a recording of a river in the Sierra foothills, a camera in this room.

A river is not only its water. It is the bed the water carves, the stones it shapes, the land that holds it. The water itself is only passing through: it has been ocean, cloud, snow and rain, and it will be again.

Here, a river in the foothills of the Sierra Nevada flows through the room. What you see was filmed at the river itself, then carried down the ropes and across the panels, slowed and quickened and coloured by the season.

Its year turns forward and never back. Winter holds the water nearly still, pale and frozen. Spring thaws it into rain and green. Summer brings the torrent and the sunlight. Autumn slows it again, rust and leaf and mud, on its way back to ice.

The river notices you. When the room fills, it warms toward summer and runs high. When everyone leaves, it cools through autumn and locks into winter, waiting for the next people to bring it back.

Winter white and pale blue, almost still · Spring thaw, rain, green · Summer torrent, gold light · Autumn slowing, rust and mud
