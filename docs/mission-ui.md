# Mission control v2: the design

For the team, to read on a phone. 23 Oct 2026. Goal: anyone on the team, in the dark gallery, can tell within **five seconds** whether the show is OK and, if not, what is wrong and what to do. Words in **bold** are from the [glossary](https://github.com/gavaiken/river-bitcube/blob/main/GLOSSARY.md).

How it was chosen: a 44-problem audit of today's pages; a status model (`mission/public/model.js`) that turns `GET /api/status` into every verdict; three built prototypes (A: one answer, then drill down; B: the room and the signal chain; C: a timeline); five judges (the artists; Will, Dave and Andi; Lilian and a Gray Area staffer; correctness; mobile and dark gallery). **A won every panel, 303 points to C's 271 and B's 269.** This is A, with what the judges asked to graft from B and C, without what they warned against.

## Principles

1. **One verdict, one cause, one action.** The headline is a sentence about the show, never a count of dots; one cause explains all its symptoms.
2. **The model decides, the page places.** Every state word, reason, control reason and "since" comes from `derive()` on the server's clock, so pages cannot disagree and a phone's clock cannot age or freshen anything.
3. **Unknown is never green and never red.** Stale data turns grey and says since when; a lost connection says so in one sentence.
4. **Word, then shape, then colour.** OK ✓ · Attention ▲ · Down ✕ · Unknown ?; colour is never the only signal, and status colours mean nothing else.
5. **Controls sit on the thing they change, say what they will do, confirm when it costs, and report on themselves.** Greyed with the model's reason when nothing can be done.
6. **Function first, art second.** A light page with river art in the season's palette behind translucent panels, a dark theme for the gallery; the art never sits under small text and never moves for anyone who asked it not to.

## Status vocabulary

**State words:** OK (green ✓), Attention (amber ▲), Down (red ✕), Unknown (grey ?). Attention covers running-but (slow, a camera down while the show is unaffected, coming up) and deliberate states ("dark on purpose"); no fifth colour. An item's level is the state of the piece itself; the band's level is what it means for the show (a down gallery camera: Down on its card, Attention on the band).

**Thresholds** (constants exported from `model.js`):

| Rule | Value |
|---|---|
| Polls (the mini) | bitcube port 5 s · seasons page 5 s · Modal 30 s · the full check 60 s |
| A source is stale | 3 missed polls: 15 s · 15 s · 90 s · 180 s; ages are `status.now − source.at` (the server's clock) |
| The page is stale | 30 s since the last answer (on every page, from one rule in `ui.js` `watchContact`, so a request that hangs counts as surely as one that fails): band Unknown, every item stale, every control off, the lost-contact sentence on Status, Lights and the System map. A request is abandoned after 8 s; coming back to the tab drops a hung request and asks again. Elapsed time is the larger of the phone's monotonic and wall clocks, so a phone that slept never reads an old answer as new (the same for the Live frame, the room and gallery stills and a calibration's time left; the Live map dims while contact is lost). The verdict flips by the same unrounded > 30 s rule as the header, in the same second. Before the first answer the header says "No answer yet · 13 s", counted from when the page started asking (amber from 12 s, red from 30 s), never "Updated N s ago" |
| TouchDesigner slow | `td.fps` under 30 |
| Coming up | bitcube up under 15 min, or `fps` null on the first check after a start |
| Dave's agent late | `next_tick_epoch` more than 15 **daytime** minutes past (07:00–23:55 Pacific); overnight is "Resting" |
| Clips failing | the last good clip line older than 5 min |
| Disk low | under 20 GB |
| **Intensity** | stillness < 0.1 · trickle < 0.3 · flowing < 0.6 · strong < 0.8 · torrent |
| Headline order | down > warning > unknown > ok, then the cause's priority (page stale, bitcube offline, check failed, TouchDesigner frozen, the lights, …) |

**Freshness rule:** one ticking age in the header (the phone's clock, only for its own last fetch); one muted "from bitcube · 20 s ago" per card; the band mentions freshness only when it is the problem ("Mission control hasn't heard from bitcube for 3 min"). Ages past 2 min turn amber. A fresh Down beats a stale Unknown. Not stale: the full check while bitcube is offline, the seasons page while the live river runs.

**A single cause explains several symptoms** (`problems[]`, `item.cause`):

| Cause | Owns | Headline → action |
|---|---|---|
| bitcube offline | everything behind it, one folded line | "bitcube is offline: nothing behind it can be checked" → power light, the router, wait a minute |
| TouchDesigner frozen / not running | the seasons page, the ropes, both boxes, the panels | "TouchDesigner is frozen: the lights are not being driven" → answer the dialog, or Switch now |
| the lights aren't connected | the ropes, both boxes, the panels | → in the ceiling: the lighting switch into bitcube's LIGHTS port, HDMI into the panel sender (Lilian) |
| no internet | clips to Dave, the river camera, the recorder | → check the router's uplink (Will); clips resume by themselves |
| coming up | TouchDesigner, a missing Modal link | "bitcube restarted 6 min ago and is still coming up" → wait two minutes |
| a leftover unknown | its own cause | an all-green page can never hide an unchecked piece |

**Since when** comes from the server (`lib/history.mjs`, transitions only), in duration form so a first sighting never reads as the fault's onset: "Down for 9 min", "for over 2 h". Never the page's own watch start. A "since" is hidden when it would contradict the words beside it: longer than bitcube has been up (for what bitcube reports), a first sighting ("over …") next to a duration the headline or summary states itself, or shorter than that duration. A failing source keeps when its failures began (`failing_since`), so "its full check has failed for 52 min" grows. On a row from a last answer (stale, or contact lost) the since stops where the item was last seen: "Down for over 24 min when last seen", never a duration nobody observed.

**Sentences.** Sentence case. Banned in any headline, summary, chip, action or control reason (the model's test enforces it): fps, TCP, SSH, VNC, RTSP, PSAC10, probe, OSError, Art-Net, WMI, HTTP, JSON, null, undefined, epoch, toe, "deployed", step ids, a trailing "?". A number in a summary only when it *is* the reason; settings with units ("100 %"); measurements in the detail; ages always. 24-hour Pacific, "today / yesterday"; durations from one `dur()`. One arrow, "→", only for the path to a control. Reasons are chosen upstream-first (bitcube online? check answered? TouchDesigner answering? which file? internet?), never guessed. The headline's first top-level ":" or ";" splits it into a big cause and a smaller consequence, never inside parentheses.

The band's verdict the restart interrupted, the same after it and about something the restart doesn't touch (a camera, the internet; not TouchDesigner, the ropes, the panels or the year's page), keeps its since from before the restart, never "Attention for 33 s" beside a camera "Down for over 31 min", and Recent says "After the restart, as before: the gallery camera is down; …" (R8 #5).

**Logins never reach a page or the history.** A camera's RTSP address carries its login, and ffmpeg prints it when the camera fails: calibrate.py and capture.py strip it from every log line, and mission control strips any `scheme://user:password@` from what it stores and serves (`lib/scrub.mjs`). The camera's HTTP API takes its login as a JSON field or a query and answers its token as one (`{"Token":{"name":…}}`), so the value of every `password`, `passwd`, `pwd`, `passphrase`, `token`, `secret` or `api_key`, also with a prefix (`cam_password`, `access_token`, `X-Auth-Token`, `CLAUDE_CODE_OAUTH_TOKEN=`; a JSON or escaped-JSON field, a query or form parameter, "key: value"), and a command line's login forms (an `Authorization:` header's credential, `Bearer …`, `curl -u user:pw`, `--password pw`, `sshpass -p pw`: R8 #38) are masked too, there and in `probe.ps1` Mask-Creds, with the same patterns (R7 #37): a quoted value to its own closing quote, past an escaped quote inside it; an object or an array whole (one cut off, or nested over 4 deep, to the end of its line); a bare value in JSON text as `"***"`, so the JSON stays JSON; `null`, `true` and `false` stay. In an object, every value under one of those keys (a prefix allowed; never `input_tokens`, `max_tokens`, `passwords` or `secret_ref`) is `"***"` whatever it holds (CR7 #1–3, R8 #38).

## Applying a change

Every control is pessimistic: nothing on screen claims a change until bitcube's own reading shows it. The same pattern on every page, every phone and the server (Gavin, 25 Oct: "standard UX patterns for async tasks and blocking editing settings while a change is being applied").

**States of one control** (`ui.js control()`, and status.js `runControl` on the same rules):

| State | When | On screen |
|---|---|---|
| ready | nothing in flight | the button, its one-line effect, the current value |
| blocked | the model says it can't run now (offline, a calibration, **another change being applied** anywhere) | greyed (dashed, full opacity), "Not possible now: <why>." *above* the buttons; inputs disabled too |
| confirm | a costly change was pressed | the sheet names the cost; focus on the safe button. Withdrawn if the control becomes blocked while it is open: "Not sent at 21:17: the switch to the live river was being applied. Press again when it is done." Focus then goes to the control's own "Wait: …" / "Not possible now: …" line, never an empty line or the page |
| applying | the POST is in flight | spinner + "Applying: <what>…"; the control, its siblings **and its inputs** disabled; the host `aria-busy="true"`; long changes (expected over 20 s: switch, rebuild, calibrate) show elapsed m:ss and the usual time ("usually about 1 min"; half minutes, number and unit never split); a short change past its usual time shows its m:ss and "usually a few seconds" |
| confirming | the POST answered OK, but the change is only real when the server confirms it: a switch, a restart, a rebuild, a boot-file change, a lighting port change (R8 #31), a calibration's start (Lights' Start and Dry run) | "Sent · waiting for bitcube to show it · 0:34 · usually about 1 min" (a start: "… · a run usually shows within a minute"; said once); still locked. The server keeps the lock too (below) |
| done | the server's verdict: its entry (by the `applying_id` the answer named, else by the `request_id` the page sent with the press: model.js `entryIdOf`, R8 #2) is in `status.confirmed`, or after its 10 minutes in the history's `status.verdicts` (the last 20 changes from the last 12 h, across a restart of the server: R8 #0). Never from the reading's words: a restart names the same file before and after, and a full check from before the restart can land after it (R7 #6, #25). When that verdict can't be known (no id at all; no list names it), a switch to a file other than the one running when it was sent is done once a full check taken after the send (its `at`, on the server's clock) shows TouchDesigner answering on that file, and a boot-file change to another boot file once one shows it; a restart (the same file) or a rebuild never, since no reading can tell (model.js `readingShows`, `serverJudged`; CR7 #4). For a control whose own answer is bitcube's reading (ropes, panels, mode, target, rules), the answer itself | "Now <state> · 21:04"; inputs unlock; drafts clear. It gives way to a newer reading that no longer shows it |
| unconfirmed | the server gave up on it (its entry is in `status.expired`; one that was "may or may not have happened" then says "… was sent at 21:04, but bitcube doesn't show it yet", once), or no verdict within this page's own limit (3 × the usual time, at least 2 min, counted from the answer as the server counts its lock), judged only from a reading taken past the limit (never without contact, never by the clock alone after the phone wakes); also an answer that it may or may not have happened: a server answer `{ ok: false, maybe: true }` (bitcube didn't answer it in time; it still names its entry), no answer in time, one that can't be read, a proxy's error page, a request lost at the network level | gave up: "The switch to the live river was sent at 21:04, but bitcube doesn't show it yet: look at the Now line, or call Gavin."; a maybe: "bitcube didn't answer in time: it may or may not have happened; the Now line shows what bitcube reports (21:04)." ("No answer in 1 min: …", "Mission control didn't answer: …" for the others). Unlocks. Never "unchanged". A later `status.confirmed` naming it (by its id, or by its request id when the answer was lost) turns it into done, a quick change's too. A maybe whose lock still holds (a switch, rebuild, boot-file or port change, a calibration's start: model.js `MAYBE_HOLDS`) says one state, when the buttons unlock by its entry's limit: "The switch to the live river got no answer from bitcube in time: it may or may not have happened; the buttons unlock when bitcube shows it, or by 21:07.", and its control shows no "Wait: …" line about its own change (`controls.<name>.by` is its entry; on Lights neither does Dry run under its own Start, R8 #18) |
| failed | refused or failed | "<thing> unchanged: <reason in words> (21:04). Try again, or see the verdict above." (Lights: "… Try again, or check Status."); another change in flight: "… was being applied (started 21:04). Press again when it is done." The draft stays; focus stays on the control |

One page-level polite live region announces applying, confirming, done, unconfirmed and failed once each; ticking times are never announced (the band's and Lights' hidden regions say their words without the times); every region speaks through one queue, never two in the same moment, and into an open sheet's own region while one is up (R8 #8, #11: "Screen readers"). A control or box that becomes blocked while it has focus hands focus to its own visible "Wait: …" line and takes it back when it unlocks.

**Locks across phones.** The server records every control in flight in `status.applying`: `[{ id, request_id?, action, label, file?, source ('mission control' | 'studio chat'), phase ('applying' | 'confirming'), started_at, answered_at?, maybe?, expect_s, blocks: [action…] }]` (`request_id`: the page's own id for the press, sent in every control POST's body as `request_id`, so a page whose answer was lost on the way back still finds its entry and its verdict, R8 #2) (`label` is the change's one name, made by model.js `changeLabel` for the server's entry, this phone's own applying line, every other phone's band, Last done and the unconfirmed line, R7 #19: "the switch to the live river", "the restart on the river's year", "the rebuild of the river's year", "the change of the boot file to the river's year", "the change of the ropes to dark", "the change of the ropes to the river", "the change of the panels to 90 %", "the change of the mode to Manual", "the change of the target to summer, strong", "the change of 2 rules", "the reset of the rules", "the change of the lighting port to the spare router port", "the calibration start", "the dry run start" (and in Last done and Recent "the calibration stop", "the dry run stop"; R8 #20, #21); a dry run's start, or a decode of earlier photos, also carries `dry: true`; `started_at` is the server's clock; `id` is unique across restarts of the server). Every control answer, a maybe answer too, names its entry: `applying_id`, so a page knows which entry is its own.

**The server is the one authority on when a change is done** (R7). An entry ends one of three ways: failed (its request failed or was refused: just removed), confirmed, or expired. Confirmed: its own good answer was the change (ropes, panels, the year's controls, a boot file or a lighting port the answer's own reading shows, a calibration whose run was already in the answer), or bitcube's reading showed it: a switch or a rebuild stays (phase `confirming`, still blocking) until a full check asked after its answer shows TouchDesigner answering on its file, a boot-file change until one shows its boot file (an OK answer whose reading still names the old boot file proves nothing: probe.ps1's write can fail silently, R8 #32), a lighting port change (bitcube didn't answer it in time, or its answer reads another port) until one shows its port (R8 #31), and a calibration start held as "starting" until its run's progress shows. Expired: its confirm limit (3 × the usual time, at least 2 min, from the answer) passed with none. The server keeps both for 10 minutes, `status.confirmed` and `status.expired`: `[{ id, request_id?, action, label, source, file?, started_at, at, maybe? }]`, the newest 100 (a burst of quick changes never grows every phone's poll without end: the oldest one confirmed at its answer goes first, R8 #39), so every phone (and the one that sent it, after a sleep) sees the server's verdict, and the history keeps it for longer (`status.verdicts`, above); a page confirms its own switch, restart, rebuild, boot-file change or calibration start only by its id there (`model.js entryVerdict`), never from a file name. The server asks the full check again 15 s and 45 s after a switch or rebuild answers. A full check is kept only when it was asked after the reading already kept (a control's own reading counts as of its answer), so a check asked before a restart and landing after it never puts TouchDesigner back on the file it is restarting on. The map in use (`GET /api/calibration` `rebuilt`) is recorded only when the rebuild is confirmed, never at its answer. Meanwhile the model reads TouchDesigner not running or not answering as "TouchDesigner is restarting on <file>" (Attention), never Down or "press Switch". A switch or rebuild bitcube didn't answer in time explains nothing (below), but while it holds the lock the advice for a TouchDesigner seen not running or not answering never names a button the lock greys: "The restart on the river's year got no answer from bitcube at 21:04; if TouchDesigner still isn't running when the buttons unlock (about 21:07), press Switch to the river's year below (about a minute).", the band's jump button gives way to its "Wait: …" line, and the Show file readout says "Maybe restarting on the river's year: bitcube didn't answer in time" (model `show.held`, R8 #16). A change bitcube didn't answer in time (`maybe`) keeps its lock until its confirm limit when it could still be under way (a switch, a rebuild, a boot-file change, the lighting port, a calibration's start); a restart or a rebuild that got no answer is confirmed only after TouchDesigner was seen not answering after it (the old TouchDesigner answering on the same file proves nothing), and it explains nothing about TouchDesigner being down. Another phone's change reads on the band "Applying: <label> · 0:23 · usually about 1 min" or "Waiting for bitcube to show <label> · …" (a calibration start "· a run usually shows within a minute", a boot-file change "· bitcube's full check shows it within a minute", as the sending phone says them: R8 #22), one bitcube didn't answer "No answer in time for <label>: waiting for bitcube to show whether it happened · 1:15" (on Status too, every second: R8 #17), with "· from the studio chat" for the agent's; the same lines on Lights under its state line and in the studio chat over its pinned bar. While the band's line says the last action's own entry, its "Last …" line is left out, so one change is never said twice in one card; it comes back as "Last done" or "Last sent … didn't show it in time" when the entry ends (R8 #23). When another phone's change ends, the band's, Lights' and the chat bar's hidden regions say once how: "Done: the boot file change to the live river, from the studio chat." or "The switch to the live river: bitcube didn't show it in time." (for 30 s after it ended, a change that waited for the server's verdict, never this phone's own; model.js `endedSaid`, R8 #9). This phone's own change is said on its control only: its entry, by id, never shows on the band or Lights' state line while its control is applying, confirming or unconfirmed; without an id, by its action: a request still in flight or confirming, and, once its control gave up on it (unconfirmed: no answer in time), an entry of that action begun before it did (one begun later is another phone's; model.js `ownEntries`, CR7 #10). A POST that conflicts with an entry gets **409** `{ ok: false, error: "wait: <label> is being applied (started 21:04)" }`; the pages show the server's words as they are. The model turns each entry into `controls.<name>.why = "wait: <label> is being applied"` for every action in its `blocks`, on every phone, from the next `/api/status` (a page that just sent a change refreshes at once). What blocks what:

| In flight | Blocks |
|---|---|
| switch, rebuild | everything that touches TouchDesigner or the year: ropes, hub75, mode, target, rules, rules-reset, lightsroute, switch, rebuild, bootfile, calibrate |
| bootfile | switch, rebuild, bootfile, calibrate |
| lightsroute | ropes, hub75, lightsroute, calibrate |
| ropes, hub75, mode, target, rules, rules-reset | the same action (two phones changing one thing at once) |
| a calibration starting (the start request) | ropes, hub75, switch, lightsroute, rebuild, calibrate; a dry run's start (or a decode of earlier photos) only a second start, nothing waits for it, and it waits only for another start (CR7 #6, #7) |
| a calibration running (`calibration.running`, not stale) | ropes, hub75, switch, lightsroute, rebuild (`CAL_BLOCKED`); a dry run or a decode of earlier photos nothing (they touch no lights: R7 #20); and a calibration can't start while a switch, rebuild or boot-file change is in flight. Stop is never blocked, on the pages or for the studio chat |

The server takes a started calibration's state from Lights' own answer at once (the probe's copy is up to a minute old), so the lock holds from the first second of the run, on the server and in `/api/status`.

**Forms** (Rules, Target, panel brightness): the unit sits beside every box ("240 s"); a value outside the range says so under its box ("Must be 60 to 1800 s") with `aria-invalid`, and Apply stays disabled; Apply is enabled only when something changed and everything is valid, and says how many ("Apply 2 changes"); the inputs lock while applying; a failure keeps what was typed; a confirmed change clears the draft. Another phone's change arriving while you edit never overwrites what you typed: the box keeps your draft and shows "now N on bitcube" under it. The box being typed in is never re-created: a re-render while you type (the year moving on changes the Steer section every few seconds live) draws everything around it anew and keeps that box itself, its caret and its focus, so "150" never becomes "015" (ui.js `morphKeeping`, R8 #29).

**On Status** every control runs on these states (`status.js` on `model.js` `ctlStep`/`ctlView`, words in `status-words.js`): one status line per control (not a live region; its m:ss ticks without re-rendering), the page asks `/api/status` again right after a send, and the band carries a quiet "Applying: the switch to the live river · 0:23 · usually about 1 min" for a change in flight from another phone or the chat (not this page's own, known by its entry's id). The show-file switch and the boot file are done on the server's verdict (`serverConfirms`): "Now showing the river's year" only once `status.confirmed` names the restart's entry. A switch also sets the boot file (probe.ps1 writes `boot-file.txt`), so its confirm and result say so when the boot file changes ("Now showing the live river; it also opens at boot from now on · 21:04"); the Show file readout is "Now showing <file> · opens at boot: <file>" ("(by default)" when no boot file was chosen), and "Restarting on <file> · …" while TouchDesigner restarts, never "What is showing isn't known" (R7 #18); meanwhile its button keeps the name that was pressed, "Restart on the river's year", never "Switch to …" (R8 #10; a restart bitcube didn't answer in time keeps "Switch to …", the words its advice uses). A readout from a last answer (its item stale: an old full check, or contact lost) is in the past, never "Now": "Last seen: showing the river's year · opens at boot: the river's year · 15 min ago", "Last seen: the ropes show the river · 15 min ago", "Power when last seen: …", the panels, the lighting port and the boot file the same (R8 #33). Target's button is "Apply N changes"; Panels brightness keeps "Set 90 %". A form's failure adds "What you typed is kept." The Season card's mode words follow the year-length rule ("one season every 2.5 min"). The Installer "changes cabling and boot settings"; its ports are "Router port (spare)". `/status#r-<id>` opens that row after the first answer.

**Requests never hang the page**: every fetch (status, calibration progress, the LED map, stills, frames) has a time limit; a page that couldn't read something asks again on its own (the next good answer, or every 30 s) and says "hasn't loaded yet", never "there is none".

**Slow links** (R7 #1): text answers are gzipped when the request accepts it (the LED map 481 → 39 KB, model.js 100 → 33 KB, `/api/status` 6 → 2 KB); the page files and the pages revalidate (`ETag`, `Last-Modified`, `cache-control: no-cache`: a 304 when unchanged, never to someone logged out), so a tab switch downloads only what changed; every API answer stays `no-store`. Compressing never holds the server up (`lib/zip.mjs`, CR7 #12): a page file is gzipped once per ETag, a big answer (the LED map) off the event loop and once per content, a small one at once. At 400 kbps with 400 ms of latency the first verdict shows in about 4 s and the LED map in about 5 s.
